Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 29, 2026  |  Organization: Our Planet Project Foundation  |  Website: ourplanetproject.com

Our Planet Project Foundation respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us, subscribe to updates, make a donation, or otherwise interact with our organization.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you choose to provide to us, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Mailing address
  • Donation information
  • Messages or inquiries you send through our website
  • Newsletter or subscription preferences
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide

We may also collect basic technical information when you visit our website, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of visit
  • Referring website
  • General location information

This technical information may be collected through cookies, analytics tools, server logs, or similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Process donations
  • Send newsletters or updates, if you have subscribed
  • Provide information about our projects, campaigns, research, events, or initiatives
  • Improve our website and communications
  • Maintain records for administrative, legal, or financial purposes
  • Protect the security and integrity of our website
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations

We do not collect more personal information than we reasonably need for these purposes.

3. Donations and Payment Processing

If you make a donation through our website, payment information may be processed by a third-party payment provider. Our Planet Project Foundation does not store full credit card numbers or complete payment credentials on our own servers.

Payment providers may collect and process your information according to their own privacy policies and security practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any payment platform used during the donation process.

4. Email Communications

If you subscribe to our newsletter or updates, we may use your email address to send information about Our Planet Project Foundation, including project updates, educational content, fundraising campaigns, events, or related initiatives.

You may unsubscribe from promotional or newsletter emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in our emails or by contacting us directly.

We may still send necessary administrative messages, such as donation confirmations or responses to direct inquiries.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, understand visitor activity, and improve our content.

Cookies are small files stored on your device. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the website may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

We may use analytics tools, such as Google Analytics or similar services, to help us understand how visitors use our website. These tools may collect information such as pages visited, time spent on the site, browser type, and general geographic region.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited personal information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our website, manage communications, process donations, provide analytics, host data, or perform administrative functions.

These service providers are only permitted to use personal information as necessary to provide services to us.

We may also disclose personal information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect our rights, safety, property, users, donors, or the public.

7. International Visitors

Our Planet Project Foundation may receive visits, inquiries, or donations from individuals outside Canada.

If you access our website from outside Canada, your information may be processed in Canada or in other jurisdictions where our service providers operate. Privacy laws in those jurisdictions may differ from the laws where you live.

Where applicable, individuals in certain regions, including the European Union, may have additional privacy rights under local law. The GDPR recognizes rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, and rights relating to automated decision-making.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Donation, financial, and administrative records may be retained for legal, accounting, tax, and audit purposes.

9. How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information against loss, theft, unauthorized access, disclosure, copying, use, or modification.

However, no website, email system, or online transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information entrusted to us.

10. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Withdraw consent to certain uses of your information
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to legal or administrative obligations

  • Ask questions about how your information is collected, used, or stored

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the information below.
