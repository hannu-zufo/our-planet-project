Biography

The Scholar Behind the Thesis

John Ward has dedicated his career to the intersection of atmospheric science and global nuclear security policy. Though 20,000 hours of rigorous, fact-oriented research, Mr. Ward painstakingly investigated systemic vulnerabilities and historic near-miss events. Analyzing the information acquired led to his argument that nuclear deterrence was never a reliable safeguard but an astonishing product of wishful thinking instead.

Mr. Ward's approach toward knowledge was different. After immersing himself in the works of the world's top authorities on nuclear history, modern geopolitics, digitizing nuclear command and control systems, basic nuclear physics, and coming nuclear weapons systems, Mr. Ward repeatedly analyzed the experts' arguments against each other in order to determine who made sense, who did not, and why. The experience proved invaluable when a broad range of contemporary knowledge was distilled into a single definitive account.

Mr. Ward's seminal monograph, ROLLING THE DICE WITH NUCLEAR WEAPONS: The Illusion of Control and the Path Forward, combines extensive research into contemporary climate modelling with in-depth analysis of actual nuclear and near-miss events. Synthesizing these elements with other risk factors generated a unique perspective that nuclear experts with the Council on Foreign Relations called, "A strong cautionary note at a moment when regional and global confrontations are becoming more prevalent."

Institutional Roles

John Ward is the founder and senior fellow at Our Planet Project Foundation, a non-partisan research and statistical analysis organization dedicated toward translating academic findings into realistic policy for mitigating nuclear risks. Mr. Ward established the Foundation in 2017, after a decade of research revealed not only systemic but randomly oriented flaws in nuclear deterrence doctrine and weapons systems safeguards. He is available for presentations, interviews, lectures, or academic discussions, while also organizing an educational tour of North America to speak about nuclear weapons and his original plan for nuclear disarmament.
